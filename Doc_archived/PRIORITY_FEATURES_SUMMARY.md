## 🥇 #1: Mobile Money Integration
**Impact: 🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥 CRITICAL**

### Why?
Most Liberians don't have credit cards but use:
- Orange Money (77, 76)
- MTN Mobile Money (88, 86)
- Lonestar (77, 44)

### What to Build:
```
Payment Flow:
1. Buyer makes offer → Seller accepts
2. Buyer gets payment request on phone
3. Buyer authorizes payment via mobile money
4. Funds held in escrow
5. After meetup/delivery → Release to seller

Cost: 2% transaction fee
Revenue: Your platform takes 1%
```

**Estimated Dev Time: 2-3 weeks**
**Revenue Impact: High $$$$**
