       startListening(onResult, onError, lang='en-US')  // Start listening

       setDefaultLang(lang)                // Set app language

       getAvailablePrompts()               // List available voice messages

       Specific Illiterate User Workflows:

       Finding Products (Voice Search):
       1. See large "Browse All" button on home
       2. Tap button → Products page
       3. See large microphone button in search bar
       4. Tap microphone → App says "Please say your search query"
       5. User says "Cassava" or "Phone"
       6. Products matching query appear
       7. Tap speaker button on product card to hear details
       8. Tap product to see full information with voice button

       Selling Products (Voice Form):
       1. From home: Tap "I Am a Seller" → Register with phone + voice input for name
       2. After login: Tap "Sell Item" from dashboard
       3. Each form field has microphone button

       5. User speaks: "Good used phone"
       6. Tap read-back button (🔉) to hear confirmation
       7. Repeat for description, category selection, price, location
       8. Tap submit → "Done. Success" + redirects to dashboard

       Buying Products:
       1. See "Browse All" button on home
       2. Tap to view all products
       3. Tap speaker button on any product to hear price, location, seller
       4. Tap product to see full details
       5. Tap "Contact Seller" to call/message
